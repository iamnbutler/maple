## Tooltip decomposition order — SOLVED

The parenthetical on a GMS tooltip is **`(base +starforce +flame)`**, not
`(base +flame +starforce)`. Established by comparing the captured numbers
against `starforce.cumulativeStarStats`, which reproduces the MIDDLE component
exactly on every item checked:

| item | engine star gain | tooltip |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Warrior Helm 17★ L150 | stat 62, ATT 19, HP 255 | `STR +142 (40 +62 +40)` · `ATT +27 (2 +19 +6)` · `Max HP +615 (360 +255)` |
| Fafnir Soaring Sword 14★ L150 | stat 37, ATT 62, HP 230 | `STR +125 (40 +37 +48)` · `ATT +275 (171 +62 +42)` · `Max HP +230 (0 +230)` |
| Superior Gollux Belt 18★ L150 | stat 73, ATT 30, HP 255 | `STR +149 (60 +73 +16)` · `ATT +65 (35 +30)` · `Max HP +455 (200 +255)` |
| Daybreak Pendant 16★ L140 | stat 49, ATT 8, HP 255 | `STR +81 (8 +49 +24)` · `ATT +16 (2 +8 +6)` · `Max HP +255 (0 +255)` |
| Dominator Pendant 17★ L140 | stat 58, ATT 17, HP 255 | `STR +138 (20 +58 +60)` · `ATT +20 (3 +17)` · `Max HP +255 (0 +255)` |

This is a second independent validation of the engine: the star-force stat
tables in `src/lib/data/starforce.ts` reproduce a real client, item by item.

All 17 transcribed decompositions sum exactly to their totals — 0 inconsistent
stat lines across every stat key. `statBreakdown()` in `src/lib/ui/slots.ts`
flags any that do not, so a future mis-transcription shows up in the UI.
